Privacy Policy

SAY AI · Developed by TR Official Team · Last updated: 12 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SAY AI ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use the SAY AI website or mobile application. By using SAY AI, you acknowledge this policy.

1. Information that may be processed

  • Account information: information such as your email address and account identifier when you sign in.
  • Messages and content: prompts, chat messages, conversation history, and files or images that you choose to submit.
  • Voice and image inputs: audio, microphone input, images, or other media only when you choose to use a feature that requires them and grant the relevant device permission.
  • App and device information: basic technical information, diagnostics, and logs needed to operate, protect, and troubleshoot the service.
  • Provider settings: AI provider preferences and API configuration information that an administrator or user chooses to add to the App.

2. How information is used

Information may be used to provide chat, voice, image-generation and other requested features; maintain conversation history and account access; manage AI provider connections; secure the service; troubleshoot errors; and respond to support requests.

3. AI providers and service partners

When you submit a prompt, message, audio recording, image, or file to an AI feature, the relevant content may be sent to the AI provider selected or configured for that feature so it can generate a response. Those providers process information under their own privacy policies and terms. SAY AI may also use infrastructure and database services, including Supabase and Cloudflare, to operate the App. Information is shared with these services only as needed to provide and protect the requested service.

Please do not submit passwords, payment-card details, government identity numbers, or other highly sensitive information in prompts or uploads.

4. Storage and retention

Account details, settings, and conversation data may be stored by the services used to operate SAY AI, depending on the features you use and the application's configuration. Information is kept for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, meet legitimate operational needs, resolve disputes, and comply with applicable law. Retention practices of third-party AI providers are governed by their own policies.

5. Your choices and rights

You can choose what content to submit and whether to use optional microphone, camera, or file-upload features. You can revoke device permissions in your Android or browser settings. You may contact us to ask about access, correction, or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable law and technical or legal limitations.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information. No internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Keep your account credentials and any API keys confidential.

7. Children's privacy

SAY AI is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

8. Third-party services and links

The App may connect to third-party AI providers or services and may contain links to their websites. Their privacy practices are controlled by those providers, not by this policy. Please review their policies before using those services.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App or its practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
